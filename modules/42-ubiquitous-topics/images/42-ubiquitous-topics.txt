Welcome to module 4.2, where we focus on ubiquitous topics. In this final module, we zoom out to look at the broader operator ecosystem and explore alternative Kubernetes form factors.

We will begin by examining the operator capability model and the current framework landscape. From there, we will survey control plane architectures that diverge from the standard self-hosted model.

Our discussion will cover two main areas. First, we will look at the operator landscape. This includes the formal definition of operators, the five-level capability model, the major frameworks available today, and proven design patterns.

Second, we will cover alternative control planes and form factors. This will include hosted control planes, Kubernetes-like API platforms, and minimal distributions that are specifically built for edge computing and resource-constrained environments.
