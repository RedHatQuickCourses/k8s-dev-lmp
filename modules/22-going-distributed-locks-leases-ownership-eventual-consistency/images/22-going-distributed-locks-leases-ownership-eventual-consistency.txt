In this module, we are going to explore the challenges of working in distributed systems, specifically when multiple controller replicas are managing shared resources. 

Once you move to a distributed environment, issues like race conditions, stale caches, and split-brain scenarios are no longer just theoretical concepts; they become your daily operational reality. To help you manage these challenges, we will examine four essential layers of defense.

First, you will learn how to structure your Reconcile loop to ensure every invocation is idempotent and fully tolerant of potentially stale data. 

Next, we will cover the theory behind distributed locks and discuss why a naive approach to locking is insufficient for maintaining system correctness. 

From there, you will learn how to implement Kubernetes-native leader election using Lease objects. Finally, we will explore controller sharding, which is a powerful strategy for scaling your reconciliation processes beyond the limitations of a single active replica.
