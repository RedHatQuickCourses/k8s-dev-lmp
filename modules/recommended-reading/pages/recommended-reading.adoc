#  Recommended Reading

== Recommended Reading

To deepen your understanding of Kubernetes development and distributed systems, the following resources are recommended:

*   **Core Concepts:**
    *   *Programming Kubernetes* by Michael Hausenblas and Stefan Schimanski.
    *   *Kubernetes Operators* by Jason Dobies and Joshua Wood.
    *   *CNCF Operator Whitepaper*.
*   **Security and Observability:**
    *   *Kubernetes Security and Observability* by Brendan Creane and Amit Gupta.
*   **Distributed Systems Design:**
    *   *Designing Distributed Systems* by Brendan Burns, focusing on patterns and paradigms for scalable, reliable systems.
