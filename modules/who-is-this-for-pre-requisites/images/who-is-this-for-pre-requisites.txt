Welcome to this learning path. Before we begin, let’s identify who this content is designed for.

This curriculum is intended for technical professionals who already possess practical experience with containerized environments and Kubernetes, and who are now looking to move beyond basic usage into platform-level development.

Specifically, this content is curated for three main groups:

First, software engineers who need to extend Kubernetes functionality by building custom controllers, operators, or specialized API servers.

Second, systems administrators, including site reliability engineers and platform engineers, who are responsible for managing, scaling, and hardening complex Kubernetes environments.

And third, platform architects who are tasked with designing automated workflows or deploying alternative control plane architectures, such as HyperShift or kcp.
