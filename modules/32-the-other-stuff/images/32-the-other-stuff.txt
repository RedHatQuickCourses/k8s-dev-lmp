Welcome to this module, where we will explore the essential topics that complement the operator and controller focus of this course. These areas are critical for managing production-grade environments and include networking, service mesh, authentication, authorization, and cluster security hardening.

We have four primary areas to cover:

First, we will look at Ingress and the Gateway API. This section explains how external traffic reaches your services, tracing the evolution from the original Ingress resource to its modern, role-oriented successor, the Gateway API.

Second, we will discuss Service Mesh Fundamentals. Here, we’ll examine the challenges a service mesh solves, its core architecture, and how Istio specifically implements traffic management, security, and observability across your services.

Third, we will cover Authentication and Authorization. We will dive into how Kubernetes identifies users and determines their permissions. This includes a look at the Node authorizer and emerging Role-Based Access Control, or ReBAC, approaches.

Finally, we will focus on Security Context and Hardening. You will learn how to effectively lock down your pods using SecurityContext fields, the Security Profiles Operator, and OpenShift SecurityContextConstraints.
