In this module, we will explore several essential topics that complement the operator and controller focus of this course. We will cover networking, service mesh, authentication, authorization, and cluster security hardening.

First, we will look at the Ingress and Gateway API. This covers how external traffic reaches your services, starting with the original Ingress resource and moving to its role-oriented successor, the Gateway API.

Next, we move to service mesh fundamentals. You will learn the problems a service mesh solves, its underlying architecture, and how Istio specifically implements traffic management, security, and observability.

We will also discuss authentication and authorization. We’ll explore how Kubernetes identifies users and determines their permissions, including the Node authorizer and emerging Relationship-Based Access Control approaches.

Finally, we will examine security context and hardening. We will focus on how to lock down pods using SecurityContext fields, the Security Profiles Operator, and OpenShift SecurityContextConstraints.
