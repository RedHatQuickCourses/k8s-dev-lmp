Welcome to module 2.2, where we explore the complexities of distributed systems.

When you have multiple controller replicas managing shared resources, you move into the territory of distributed systems. At this stage, race conditions, stale caches, and split-brain scenarios are no longer just theoretical concepts; they become daily operational realities.

To help you navigate these challenges, this module examines four essential layers of defense.

First, you will learn how to structure your reconcile loop to ensure that every invocation is idempotent and fully tolerant of stale data.

Next, we will look at the theory behind distributed locks, where you will learn why naive locking strategies are often insufficient for maintaining system correctness.

From there, we will move into implementation, where you will learn how to use Kubernetes-native Lease objects to perform leader election.

Finally, we will explore controller sharding, a powerful strategy for scaling your reconciliation processes beyond the constraints of a single active replica.
