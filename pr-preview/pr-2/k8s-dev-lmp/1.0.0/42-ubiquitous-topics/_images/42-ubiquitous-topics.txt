Welcome to module 4.2, where we focus on ubiquitous topics. In this final module, we zoom out to look at the broader operator ecosystem and explore alternative Kubernetes form factors.

We will begin by examining the operator capability model and the current framework landscape. Then, we will survey control plane architectures that diverge from the standard self-hosted model.

Our discussion will cover two main areas. First, we will explore the operator landscape, including the formal definition of operators, the five-level capability model, the major frameworks available, and proven design patterns. 

Second, we will look at alternative control planes and form factors. This includes hosted control planes, Kubernetes-like API platforms, and minimal distributions designed specifically for edge and resource-constrained environments.
