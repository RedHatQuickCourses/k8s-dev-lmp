Welcome to this learning path. Let’s start by defining who this course is for and what you should know before we begin.

This content is designed specifically for engineers, system administrators, and developers who already have practical experience working with containerized environments and Kubernetes. If you have already deployed applications on Kubernetes and are looking to move beyond basic usage into the world of platform-level development, you are in the right place.

To get the most out of this material, we strongly recommend a foundational understanding of distributed systems principles. Specifically, having some familiarity with concepts like concurrency, leasing, and synchronization will make it much easier to grasp the more advanced topics we cover, such as controllers, leader election, and eventual consistency.

In terms of an ideal baseline, we recommend a level of proficiency equivalent to the Certified Kubernetes Application Developer, or CKAD certification. Alternatively, if you have completed the Red Hat DO-180 course on containers, Kubernetes, and Red Hat OpenShift, along with the DO-280 OpenShift Administration track, you should have the hands-on experience necessary to engage with these advanced topics.

Finally, before we dive into the lessons, please take a moment to review this self-assessment checklist. You should feel comfortable with these items:

First, you should be able to create and manage Deployments, Services, and ConfigMaps. You should also understand how container image building works and how to interact with registries. 

You should be comfortable reading and writing YAML manifests and have at least a basic familiarity with Go programming. Additionally, you should be able to use the `kubectl` command-line tool to inspect and debug cluster resources. 

Lastly, make sure you understand the differences between a Pod, a ReplicaSet, and a Deployment, and that you have experience working with namespaces, resource quotas, and the fundamentals of Role-Based Access Control, including Roles and RoleBindings.

If you are confident in these areas, you are ready to begin the course.
